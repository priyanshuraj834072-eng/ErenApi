Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

ErenApi is a payment verification service. A merchant connects their own Gmail inbox, and ErenApi looks in it for FamPay payment notification emails to confirm that a payment really arrived. This policy explains what information we handle and why.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: the email address of your Google sign-in and a unique account ID.
  • API key: stored as a one-way hash for checking requests, and as an encrypted copy so you can see your key again in your dashboard.
  • Settings you add: your UPI ID and payee name, used to build payment QR codes.
  • Gmail connection: if you choose to connect Gmail, we store your Gmail address and an access token that lets us read your mail. The token is stored encrypted.
  • Payment details: when a payment email matches a verification or order request, we store the amount, payer name, transaction ID, UTR and payment time. We use them to show your payment history and to make sure the same payment is never used twice.
  • Orders: the amount, the reference you send, the status and the times of orders you create.
  • Technical logs: basic request and error logs used for security and fixing problems.

2. How we use Google user data (Gmail)

  • Permission we ask for: read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly).
  • What we use it for: only to search your inbox for FamPay payment notification emails (sent from FamPay's own domain) that match a payment you ask us to verify, and to read the amount, payer name, transaction ID, UTR and time from them.
  • What we do not do: we do not read or use any other emails. We do not store the full text of your emails; we keep only the payment details listed above.
  • We do not use Gmail data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • People at ErenApi do not read your emails, except when you ask us for help and give permission, when it is needed for security, or when the law requires it.

ErenApi's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your information. We use service providers that are needed to run ErenApi: Google (Firebase Authentication and database) and Vercel (hosting). We may share information when the law requires it. Your payment details are shown only to you and returned to your own API requests.

4. Storage and security

Connections use HTTPS. Gmail access tokens are encrypted, API keys are stored as hashes, and database access is restricted so that merchants can only reach their own data. No online service can promise perfect security.

5. Retention and deletion

  • You can disconnect Gmail at any time from your dashboard. We then revoke the access and delete the stored token. You can also remove ErenApi's access in your Google Account permissions.
  • We keep your payment history and orders while your account is active so that the service works. To delete your account and data, email us at the address below and we will do it within a reasonable time.

6. Your choices

You decide whether to connect Gmail. You can regenerate or stop using your API key, remove your saved UPI ID, and disconnect Gmail whenever you like. Without a Gmail connection, payment verification will not work.

7. Children

ErenApi is for merchants who are legally able to enter into an agreement. It is not meant for children under 18.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. The latest version and its date are always on this page.
