How it works

Three steps from "I paid" to "confirmed".

1

Customer pays

They pay you on UPI and send you the UTR or transaction ID.

2

You send us the ID

Your site or bot passes that ID to ErenApi, with the amount if you want it checked.

3

You get the answer

ErenApi checks your inbox and replies: paid, or not paid.
